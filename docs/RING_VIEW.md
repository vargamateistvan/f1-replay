# Ring view

> Current reference (moved out of `AGENTS.md`). Code: `utils/lapProgress.ts`, `components/TrackRing/` (`ringModel.ts`, `lapTimeProfile.ts`, `RingLayers.tsx`).

Inspired by MultiViewer's "Circle of Doom": a dial spaced by **time**, so the angle between two cars is their gap and a pit stop is a fixed slice of the circle.

- The backdrop uses a subtle theme-aware glow and two concentric guide rings (`MapBackground`), behind the data and included in SVG snapshots.
- N/E/S/W labels and the outer tick ring use the selected circuit geometry's `rotation` (the same broadcast orientation as the 2D map); north is red, and reversing lap direction does not rotate them.
- Mouse-wheel scrolling zooms the ring around its centre, using the same wheel sensitivity and zoom limits as the 2D map; the wheel is prevented from scrolling the page while over the ring.
- Startup: `RaceWeekend` gates the focused-car telemetry window with the optional `telemetryEnabled` prop (default true), so HUD speed/gear requests cannot compete with essential replay data and the visible GPS window.
- Each car's interpolated position is projected onto the nearest outline *segment* (not vertex) of the 2D map's outline → lap (distance) fraction 0–1. The last matched segment per car is the next search hint (±`RING_SEARCH_WINDOW` segments), which also keeps cars on their own stretch where a track crosses itself; a match further than `RING_OFF_TRACK_M` falls back to a full scan.
- Time spacing (`mapRingTimeScale`, default on; `lapTimeProfile.ts`, `updateReferenceProfile` in `ringModel.ts`): every `RING_REF_REFRESH_MS` of playhead, each car's last `RING_REF_TRACE_WINDOW_MS` is traced and its full laps (two start-line crossings, no pit samples or data gaps) are added to a per-session lap pool. The dial's *shape* is the per-point median of the `RING_REF_POOL_MAX_LAPS` fastest pooled laps finished by the playhead, keeping those within `RING_REF_LAP_SPREAD` of the fastest, so corners, sectors and the pit lane stay put; safety-car, formation, traffic and odd GPS laps never reshape it. The reference *lap time* (seconds per turn of the dial) follows the cars' latest laps; a pace more than `RING_REF_PACE_MAX_SLOWDOWN` slower doesn't replace one measured within `RING_REF_PACE_HOLD_MS`. Before the first full lap the dial is spaced by distance. `toDisplay` / `displayArc` (`ringModel.ts`) apply the profile and the flip (`mapRingReverse`) to everything drawn: cars, corners, sectors, pit lane.
- Pit lane: a car more than 5 m off the line and closer to the baked pit-lane path (`data/pitLanes.ts`) than to the track goes on the inner arc, at `entry + laneFraction × span`. Without a baked path, cars > `RING_OFF_TRACK_M` off the line drop to a dashed inner circle. Never on the coarse `layout` fallback outline (not to scale).
- The optional Pit lane status setting (off by default) colours the lane arc for an explicit lane-wide report and marks the respective endpoints when a pit path is available. The exit signal uses one red or green lamp in a start-light-style housing. These are last-reported states, which may be stale if later updates are missing; unknown states stay neutral.
- Racing-driver dots and their numbers shrink by `TRACK_PIT_CAR_SCALE` on the pit arc; special vehicles retain their usual size.
- Races: labels carry the gap to the car ahead, the leader, or the focused car (`mapRingGapMode`: `"interval" | "leader" | "focus"`; the toolbar button cycles them, "focus" only with a focused car that has timing, else it reads as "interval"). Gap to the focused car (`formatFocusGap`) comes from the gap-to-leader difference: `+` behind it, `−` ahead, `+1L` a lap down. When a lapped driver's timing row includes seconds, labels retain both values (for example `+1L · +2.4s`); the seconds are the numeric interval supplied for that car. Only `RaceWeekend`'s current leader (position timeline) reads LEAD — OpenF1 reports several cars at 0 s during pit cycles, and those stale zeros are also dropped before the pit rejoin projection.
- Pit window (focused car, races, `mapShowPitRejoin`), as MultiViewer's: a purple band on the ring from the rejoin point (car's time fraction − pit loss / reference lap) to the car, with the projected position (`pitRejoinView`), and a fainter band the same length ahead. Cars that would pass it if it pitted now, and cars ahead that would drop behind it if *they* pitted (`pitWindowRivals`, by gap to leader), stay bright; the rest dim. Hatched orange ticks mark the pit loss under the other conditions (`alternativePitLosses`: SC / VSC / GREEN). Distance dial: the band runs back to where the car was `loss` seconds ago, without the ahead band or ticks.
- Pit window outcomes (`mapRingShowPitWindowOutcomes`, default off): when enabled and a rejoin projection is available, a compact overlay names the drivers who would pass the focused driver if they pit now and those who would drop behind if they pitted. It lists up to three acronyms per group, then a count of the remainder.
- Rejoin from the pits (focused car in the pit lane, time dial only): `findPitEntry` scans the car's samples back (up to `RING_PIT_ENTRY_LOOKBACK_MS`; longer reads as a garage stop) for when it left the track. Where it would be had it stayed out (entry time fraction + elapsed / reference lap) minus the pit loss is the rejoin point, which moves with the field. Its gap to the leader is read off the nearest on-track car with a gap (`gapAtTimeFraction`), then ranked (`rankAtGap`); the ring shows a dashed ghost tagged "REJOIN P{n}" (no bands or condition ticks) and the cars it would rejoin between stay bright. With the outcomes overlay on, it names those two cars and the margins. The entry is cached per stop (`cachedPitEntry`); the in-pit maths (`inPitRejoin`) lives in `pitWindow.ts`, shared with the 2D / 3D maps.
- 2D and 3D maps show the same pit window (`components/TrackMap/usePitWindow.ts`, which reuses the ring model and reference lap without drawing the ring): the band behind ends at the existing rejoin ghost (where the car was `loss` seconds ago, on its own trail), condition ticks are where it was under the other losses, the fainter band ahead and the in-pit "REJOIN P{n}" ghost come from the reference lap (`distanceAtTime` maps the time back onto the outline). Rivals keep full brightness and get a purple dashed ring instead of dimming.
- Driver inspection: focusing or hovering a car keeps it and the nearest `RING_NEARBY_CARS_PER_SIDE` cars in each direction at full emphasis, fading the rest. Pit-window rivals and the hovered comparison car are also kept visible.
- Gap measure: hovering a car while another is focused draws an arc between them inside the ring with the on-track time gap (`signedTimeDelta` × reference lap, the short way round). Needs the time scale.
- Legend: the `?` toolbar button opens a compact key for gap signs, lapped-car timing, the pit-window bands and condition ticks; it also explains the hover comparison and its time-spacing requirement.
- Centre: lap counter, track status and reference lap title stay in fixed positions without explanatory captions. When weather is available, a line under the reference lap suggests Soft for dry conditions or Intermediate when rain is reported, and identifies the compound of the fastest clean completed lap up to the playhead (or — until one is available). With no focused driver, the info area also shows track weather (track/air temperature, humidity, wind and rain) with compact icons (the 2D map's bottom-left Track Weather box is not shown in ring view); with a focused driver, that area instead shows position, number/name, gaps to the car ahead and behind, tyre compound/age, and speed/gear when telemetry is available.
- Labels are spread with 1D cluster merging (`layoutRingLabels`), cut at the widest empty stretch so 12 o'clock isn't special; pit cars' labels go inside.
- Sector 2/3 start at their first marshal post (as the 3D map), equal thirds without posts.
- The map mode setting is `mapViewMode` (`"2d" | "3d" | "ring"`); `migrateSettings` (persist v1) maps the legacy `mapView3d` boolean onto it.
